Why self-hosted automation wins for teams with compliance and control needs

When your workflows touch internal knowledge, customer data, or regulated processes, owning the deployment stack changes the conversation from convenience to control.

What self-hosted gives you

  • Data residency and reduced third-party exposure.
  • More predictable costs as usage grows.
  • Better auditability for internal review and compliance.
  • The freedom to deploy with your preferred stack and hosting provider.

For many teams, the right question is not “which hosted tool is fastest?” but “which deployment model gives us the least friction and the most control?” A self-hosted setup can be more resilient and more strategic over time.

That is why CHAINNE offers a path for teams that want the same modern automation experience while still keeping their data close to the systems that matter.
