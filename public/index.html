Solarpunk studio · Philippines

Build with intelligence. Automate with purpose.

Regenerative systems for everyone, so the delight of solarpunk can spread. High-performance, edge-deployed, and built to last.

I run Chainnes. The wider map of my work is at charlenecordero.com.

Mission

Spread solarpunk, and build technology for that future: tools that run on sunlight, can be fixed, and help living places.

Vision

A world people can picture and keep, where useful machines sit beside gardens, solar roofs, and the hands that repair them.

How to consult

  1. 01

    Send the scope

    A few sentences: what hurts, who it is for, and what must stay repairable.

  2. 02

    I check the fit

    If it does not match what I build, I say so in writing. No call.

  3. 03

    Then the call

    I schedule it only when the work belongs here, so we confirm the problem instead of hearing it for the first time.

  4. 04

    Agree the contract

    Scope, price, and the point where you can pause me.

  5. 05

    Build, then you keep it

    I make it. You can inspect it while it is open, then host, repair, and correct it.

Focus

What I build.

Five practices. Anyone can start. The work stays repairable, close, and possible to pause.

Full-stack development
Sites, member spaces, and the services behind them. Built so a person can host, read, and repair what they use.
Data & Analytics
Yield, water, waste, and what to tend next. The numbers stay with the people they belong to.
AI & automation
Agents that sort, draft, and wait. A person still decides before anything acts.
IoT & robotics
Sensors and small machines that stay nearby, speak lightly, and can be opened when a part wears out.
AR & VR
Overlays for a walk or a classroom that name plants, materials, and how to repair what is already there.

Frameworks

How a system gets made.

One person, from the first conversation through the day it is running.

  1. 01

    Explore

    Listen, then name the problem in plain words.

  2. 02

    Design

    Keep it repairable, private, compute-efficient, and light to run.

  3. 03

    Build

    Ship it on infrastructure a person can hold.

  4. 04

    Test

    Measure what got lighter, then choose the next loop.
