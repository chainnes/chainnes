Project concepts

Concepts in the garden

Chainnes does not have shipped client projects yet. These six are the first drawings, one for each practice. They are here so the work has a shape before it has a client.

Concept · Business intelligence

Canopy Ledger

A living dashboard for a regenerative farm or community forest: yield, water, waste, and what to tend next. The numbers stay with the people who grow the place.

Concept · Full-stack development

Tide Stack

A small public site and member space for a coastal or neighborhood restoration group in the Philippines. Pages, a shared record, and a way to ask for help.

Concept · AI & automation

Root Agent

A local-first agentic workflow that structures data, sorts repair requests, grant dates, and field notes, and waits for a human checkpoint before it acts.

Concept · IoT

Plot Node

Soil, rain, and solar micro-sensors running on a decentralized local network, emitting only the metrics the ecosystem requires via lightweight protocols.

Concept · Robotics

Seed Walker

A small nursery machine for trays and paths. Drawn to be opened, cleaned, and repaired, rather than swapped when a part wears out.

Concept · AR & VR

Grove Layer

An overlay for a city walk or a classroom. It names plants and materials, and it shows how to repair what is already there.
