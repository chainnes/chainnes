What Chainnes is

Chainnes is my studio. One person, in the Philippines, building for a solarpunk future. Solarpunk is not a style I put on a slide. It is a future of sunlight, repair, and shared living places, and I want that delight to spread to everyone, not only to a short list of organizations.

I use advanced tools. Models, machines, sensors, software. Agents help me draft and check. They do not get the last word. If I am not there, a workflow waits. That is on purpose.

The work I take has to be useful, repairable, and possible to pause. If it hides its cost, its data trail, or its waste, I decline it. You do not need a finished brief to begin. Send a few sentences: what hurts, who it is for, and what must stay repairable. I read that first. I book a call only when the work matches what I build.

This is the first post. The projects on the site are still drawings. I would rather show the shape of the practice than invent a client I do not have.
